## Recorded audio and foley

Assets under `My project (1)/Assets/_Paridhwani/Resources/VillageAudio` replace the old synthesized noise and footsteps. Source metadata, original/shipping SHA-256 hashes, processing and measured shipping-file levels are in `docs/AUDIO_ASSETS.json`.

CC0 1.0 sources (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/):

- Rain: "Soft rain.WAV", barkenov, https://freesound.org/s/640655/.
- RoofRunoff: "Rain in the Gutter Loop", Ogrebane, https://opengameart.org/content/rain-gutter-loop.
- River: "waterfall 48000 24 bit", saeedamirabadizadeh, https://freesound.org/s/749871/. Field recording of Sarkhang village waterfall, Iran; used as water ambience, not represented as a Karnataka location recording.
- Pour: "Pouring water from the kettle", Moonlight Dancer, https://freesound.org/s/213737/.
- Boiler: "Boiling water.wav", xenognosis, https://freesound.org/s/137229/.
- Bus: "Bussi_idle10", Akseli1236, https://freesound.org/s/768261/.
- Auto: "INSIDE AN AUTO RICKSHAW IN MUMBAI.mp3", ambar das, https://freesound.org/s/203177/.
- Earth/Stone/Timber footsteps, Cup and TempleBell: Kenney, Impact Sounds, https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds. Earth uses grass/soft-ground foley, Stone uses concrete; these are not claimed as dedicated wet-mud/puddle recordings.
- NotebookOpen/Close, Paper, Door, Latch and Utensil: Kenney, RPG Audio, https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio.

Bird, Cricket and Frog: Blender Foundation, "Ambient Bird, Cricket and Frog" from Yo Frankie!, submitted by Lamoot, https://opengameart.org/content/ambient-bird-cricket-and-frog. Licensed CC BY 3.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/. Changes: cropped silence, high-pass/DC filtering, level matching, mono conversion and OGG encoding. These are sparse illustrative wildlife calls; species and regional authenticity have not been established. Attribution and license links also appear in the game's credits.

Freesound recordings were acquired through the public HQ MP3 files associated with their source pages, not the account-gated original lossless WAV downloads. All shipping clips are edited/re-encoded OGG files, with source rates recorded in the manifest. No claim is made that conversion restores lossy source detail. Python/NumPy/SciPy/SoundFile/FFmpeg are preparation tools only and are not shipped with the game.
