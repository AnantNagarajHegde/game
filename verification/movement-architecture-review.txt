Scope and limits

are preserved. This is a functional/rendering verification record, not a minimum-hardware
or commercial release certification. The restricted standalone runner logs Windows

native gamepad movement/camera and UI navigation are exercised. Optional stripped URP
effects and unavailable Unity telemetry hosts also produce environment warnings.
